---
title: "Take Profit & Stop Loss"
description: "Close a position automatically when it is up or down by the percentage you choose"
---

Take profit (TP) and stop loss (SL) close a position for you when its PnL reaches your threshold. They work on both networks and keep watching while you are offline.

## Set TP/SL on a position

In your [Portfolio](/features/portfolio), click the **Settings** badge on an open position (it reads **Set TP** / **Set SL** when nothing is set).

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **TP/SL Currency** | Measure PnL in SOL or USD (Solana), or ETH or USD (Robinhood Chain). |
| **Take Profit** | Close the position when it is up this many percent. |
| **Stop Loss** | Close the position when it is down this many percent. |

Tick a box to turn that side on, then press **Save Changes**. Untick it to turn it off.

<Tip>
  The currency matters. A SOL/USDC position can be up in USD and down in SOL at the same time. Pick the currency you actually count your gains in.
</Tip>

TP and SL work on positions in your agent wallet. A position sitting in your sign-in wallet can't use them.

## Give every new position TP/SL: Auto TP/SL

Go to **Settings > General > Auto TP/SL**.

- Off by default. When it is off, you set TP/SL on each position yourself.
- When it is on, every **new** position gets the defaults below:
  - **Currency** (SOL/ETH or USD)
  - **Take Profit**: 10% by default
  - **Stop Loss**: 10% by default
- It does not change positions you already have.

<Note>
  **Copied positions don't use Auto TP/SL.** They take the TP/SL from the copied wallet's [Exit rules](/features/copy-settings#step-2-exit-rules). Whichever comes first closes the copy: your TP/SL, or the leader's own exit.
</Note>

## What happens when it triggers

1. LP Agent closes the position from your agent wallet and swaps it back (SOL on Solana; the currency you opened with on Robinhood Chain).
2. The position moves to **Historical positions** with its final PnL.
3. If you turned on notifications, you get a **Take profit hit** or **Stop loss hit** push with the result. See [Notifications](/features/notifications).
4. The position's **Logs** show each step, such as take profit queued and close success.

A TP or SL close counts as an automated action: LP Agent takes 8% of the LP fees collected. See [Fees](/fee).

<Warning>
  A stop loss limits losses but can't guarantee a price. In a fast market the close lands at whatever the price is when the transaction goes through, and each close pays swap and network costs. A very tight stop on a small position can cost more than it saves.
</Warning>
