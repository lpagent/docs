title: "Claim SOL"
description: "Get back the SOL locked in empty token accounts (Solana)"
---

Every token you have ever held left a small account in your wallet, and each one locks about **0.0015 SOL** of rent. After a lot of LPing that adds up. **Claim SOL** closes the accounts you no longer use and sends that SOL back to you.

<Frame>
  ![Claim SOL page](/images/app/sol-claim-sol.jpg)
  <em>Claim SOL: pick a wallet, check the accounts to close, and claim</em>
</Frame>

## How to claim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Claim SOL">
    Press **Claim Now** on the banner on your Portfolio. The banner shows when you have at least 0.01 SOL to claim. You can hide it for 7 days with the X.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the wallet">
    **Agent wallet**, or **Your wallet** (the wallet you signed in with, when it is connected). Each shows how much it can claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the list">
    **Accounts to close** lists every token account that will be closed and the SOL you get back from each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Press **Claim X SOL**. Your agent wallet is claimed for you. Your own wallet asks you to sign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dust clean-up

**Also clean up dust** is on by default. It **burns** tokens worth under $0.10 each and closes their accounts too, so you get that rent back as well. The page shows how many tokens that is and what they are worth in total.

<Warning>
  Burned tokens can't be recovered. Untick **Also clean up dust** if you want to keep them. Tokens worth more than the dust limit are never touched.
</Warning>

## Cost

Claim SOL is free. LP Agent takes no fee; you only pay the Solana network fee.

## Share it

After a claim you can share a card of what you got back: post it on X or Telegram, copy the image, download it, or copy the link.
