**Smart LP** is the leaderboard of liquidity providers worth following. It is the first page you see when you open LP Agent.

<Frame>
  ![Smart LP table](/images/app/sol-smart-lp-table.jpg)

</Frame>

## What it ranks

| Network | Ranked on |
| --- | --- |
| Solana | Meteora DLMM positions |
| Robinhood Chain | Uniswap V3 and V4 positions |

Switch network with the chain picker in the header. Values show in SOL, ETH or USD, following the **$** toggle.

### Fee-checked wallets only

Some wallets look great on paper because they launch a token, seed its pool and pull the liquidity, not because they earn LP fees. Smart LP hides them.

**Fee-checked** means: if a wallet's LP fees are **under 10% of its profit**, the profit came from token price (usually a token it launched itself), and the wallet is not listed. Press **How we check** on the page for the full explanation.

### Sized to your wallet (Solana)

On Solana, Smart LP shows wallets whose total profit is up to **5x your net worth** (never less than 20 SOL). The banner at the top says where your cap is. Deposit more to see bigger wallets. On Robinhood Chain the list is not capped.

## Reading the table

Switch between **Card** and **Table** view at the top right.

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **7D PnL** | Profit over the last 7 days, with a daily bar chart |
| **Win rate** | Share of closed positions that made money. Green at 70% or more, red under 50% |
| **30D PnL** / **All-time PnL** | Profit over 30 days and all time |
| **Positions** | Total positions, plus how many in the last 7 days and across how many pools |
| **Avg age** | How long the wallet usually holds a position |
| **EV** | Expected value per position: (win rate x average win) + (loss rate x average loss), where the average loss is negative |
| **Avg invested** | Average size of a position |
| **Monthly PnL** | Average profit per month |
| **Fees** | Total LP fees earned |
| **Last active** | When the wallet last traded, and since when it has been active |

Click a column header to sort (click again to flip the order), or use the sort chips in card view. The default sort is **All-time PnL**.

<Tip>
  Look at **Avg age** before you copy. Every copied position costs a swap in, a swap back and network fees. A wallet that flips positions in a few minutes makes you pay that cost again and again. The copy dialog warns you when a wallet holds for less than 18 minutes on Solana or 1 hour on Robinhood Chain.
</Tip>

## Filters

| Filter | Default |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** (Last 24h, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Any time) | Last 7 days |
| **Min Win Rate (%)** | 80 |
| **Min Positions** | 50 |
| **Min Avg Age (hrs)** | 0.2 |
| **More filters**: Min Profit, Min Monthly Profit, Min Expected Value, Min Fees Earned, First Activity Before | Empty |

Press **Filter** to apply. Your filters are saved in this browser. **Clear** resets them.

## Copy a wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wallet">
    Click a row or card to open the wallet's portfolio. Check its open and closed positions, PnL history and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Copy LP">
    The button sits next to the wallet address. It opens the [copy settings](/features/copy-settings).
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can only copy wallets on your account's network. If you already copy a wallet, its portfolio shows a pill with your copy size and exit rules instead; click it to edit.

## When copying is off for a wallet (Robinhood Chain)

On Robinhood Chain, LP Agent also checks each leader's history. If a wallet's profit does not come from LP fees, or it mostly LPs into pools of tokens it launched itself, its portfolio shows **Copy is off for this wallet** with the reason, and the copy button reads **Copy off**.

- You can't start a new copy of that wallet.
- If you already copy it, **new copies are skipped**, but your open copies still follow its exits. The wallet shows **New copies off** in your Copied Wallets list.

Separately, a single copy is skipped when the leader opens a brand-new pool for a token with no price and holds nearly all of its liquidity. Copy Logs shows it as **Leader's own token launch**.
