## What is Copy LP?

Copy LP follows a wallet you choose (the **leader**) and repeats its LP moves in your agent wallet, scaled to your size. When the leader opens a position, you open the same pool and range. When it adds, removes, claims or closes, your copy does the same.

It is the fastest LP copy trading: 90% of copies land within 3.5 seconds of the leader's move, and it runs on LP Agent's servers around the clock, with no bot or RPC node of your own.

It works on both networks:

| Network | Pools copied |
| --- | --- |
| Solana | Meteora DLMM (Spot, Curve, Bid-Ask and custom shapes) and DAMM v2 |
| Robinhood Chain | Uniswap V3 and V4 (hooked V4 pools only if you allow them) |

## How to start copying

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fund your agent wallet">
    Copies run from your [agent wallet](/account-and-wallet#your-agent-wallet). There is no separate copy wallet. Deposit SOL (Solana) or ETH (Robinhood Chain). Keep some spare for fees and gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a wallet on Smart LP">
    Look for a good win rate, fees that make up a real share of profit, and an average hold time that is not too short. See [Smart LP](/features/smart-lp).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Copy LP on its portfolio">
    The copy dialog opens with a suggested size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your rules and press Copy">
    Five short steps: **Sizing**, **Exit rules**, **What to copy**, **Protection** and **Label**. See [Copy Settings](/features/copy-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track it under Copy LP">
    The **Copy LP** page lists your copied wallets, your results and a log of every copy. See [Managing Your Copies](/features/copy-management).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets copied

| Leader does | Your copy does | Solana | Robinhood Chain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opens a position | Opens the same pool and range at your size | Yes | Yes |
| Adds liquidity | Adds the leader's amount x your copy % (capped by your max). See [How sizing works](#how-sizing-works) | Yes | Yes |
| Removes part of a position | Removes the same share of your position | Yes | Yes |
| Closes a position | Closes your copy | Yes | Yes |
| Claims fees | Claims fees on your copy | Yes | Yes |
| Compounds | Compounds your copy | Yes | Yes |
| Rebalances | Moves your copy the same way | Yes | Shows up as a close and a new open |

- Only positions the leader opens **after** you start copying are copied.
- Adds, removes and closes only apply to positions you hold a copy of.
- Your own **take profit** and **stop loss** can close a copy before the leader does. Whichever comes first wins.
- On Solana, some rebalances can't be mirrored: for example the leader moves only part of a position, or takes profit or tops up in the same move. Those are skipped. Your copy stays where it is, and Copy Logs says why.
- On Solana, some leaders open an empty position and fund it a few seconds later. Your copy opens the empty position too (it pays the position rent), then copies the funding add. If your agent wallet can't cover that add, the copy is still empty, so it is closed about a minute later and the position rent comes back to your wallet.

## How sizing works

Your size is set by two numbers in the **Sizing** step:

- **Copy Percentage**: the share of each leader position you copy. The dialog suggests one from your net worth and the leader's, so each position is the same share of your wallet as it is of theirs.
- **Max Increase Amount per Tx** (optional): a cap on each copied open and each copied add.

**Example:** Copy Percentage 80%, Max 8 SOL.

1. The leader opens with 1 SOL. You open 0.8 SOL.
2. The leader adds 29 SOL. 80% would be 23.2 SOL, so your add is capped at 8 SOL.
3. Your copy now holds 8.8 SOL.

The cap applies **per transaction**, not per position. Removes and closes are percentage based and are never capped.

With no Copy Percentage, each copy matches the leader's amount, up to your Max.

### When the max cuts the open short

If the Max cuts the open itself, your copy holds a smaller share of the leader's position than your Copy Percentage. The leader's later adds to that position are copied at that smaller share, so your copy keeps the same shape as the leader's instead of piling into one side.

**Example:** Copy Percentage 50%, Max 7 SOL.

1. The leader opens with 100 SOL. 50% would be 50 SOL, so you open 7 SOL: 7% of the leader's position.
2. Later the leader adds 10 SOL. You add 0.7 SOL (7%), not 5 SOL (50%).

Each add still stays under your Max, and never goes above your current Copy Percentage. This holds for 30 days after the open; after that, adds go back to your Copy Percentage.

## What you need in your wallet

| Network | Keep in your agent wallet |
| --- | --- |
| Solana | The copy amount, plus position rent (returned when the position closes), plus about 0.03 SOL for fees. We suggest at least **5 SOL** of net worth to copy. |
| Robinhood Chain | ETH for gas on every copy. Each copy keeps back at least 0.001 ETH to pay for its own exit. WETH does not pay gas. We suggest at least **0.08 ETH** of net worth. |

On Robinhood Chain you can fund copies in **ETH or USDG** (**Settings > General > Base Token**). A copy closes back into the currency it was opened with. On Solana, closes swap back to SOL.

<Warning>
  Copy LP carries real risk. A leader's past results don't guarantee yours: your copy lands a moment after theirs, at a slightly different price, and pays its own swap and network costs. Size small first and use a stop loss.
</Warning>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I copy more than one wallet?">
    Yes. Each copied wallet has its own settings. Make sure your agent wallet can fund all of them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I pause a wallet?">
    Nothing from that wallet is copied while it is paused, **including closes**. Your open copies stay open and keep their own take profit and stop loss. Close them yourself if you want out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was a copy not opened?">
    Open **Copy LP > Copy Logs**. Every copy that did not open shows why (low balance, price moved, token filters, meme protection and more) and usually a button to fix it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Copy LP cost?">
    Opening a copy is free. When a copy closes or claims fees because the leader did, LP Agent takes 8% of the LP fees collected. See [Fees](/fee).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I learn to pick and size copies?">
    Read [Copy LP Best Practices](/tutorials/copy-lp-best-practices) (Solana) and [Copy LP on Robinhood Chain](/tutorials/copy-robinhood-best-practices).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
