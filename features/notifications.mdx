LP Agent can send push notifications to your phone or computer, even when the tab is closed.

## Turn them on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Account">
    From the account panel, press **Settings**, then the **Account** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on System Notifications">
    Your browser asks for permission. Allow it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable this device">
    Each device subscribes separately. If you see "Notifications are on, but this device isn't receiving them yet", press **Enable on this device**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Press **Send test notification**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also press **Turn on** on the banner at the top of the **Copy LP** page.

## What you can be notified about

Every event is on by default once System Notifications is on. Switch off the ones you don't want.

| Group | Event | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Auto exits** | **Take profit hit** | Your take profit closed a position. Sent with the final PnL. |
| | **Stop loss hit** | Your stop loss closed a position. Sent with the final PnL. |
| **Copy trading activity** | **Position opened** | Your copy opened a position after your leader did. |
| | **Position increased** | Liquidity was added to a copy. |
| | **Position decreased** | Liquidity was removed from a copy. |
| | **Position closed** | Your leader exited and your copy followed. Sent with the final PnL. |
| | **Fees claimed** | Fees were claimed on a copy. |
| | **Copy failed** | A copy could not go through, for example out of gas, slippage or low balance. |
| | **Copy skipped** | Your filters turned a copy down, like the token quality floor or a blocked pool. |

You also get a push if LP Agent pauses your copies because your agent wallet is out of funds.

## iPhone and iPad

iOS only sends push notifications to apps on your Home Screen:

1. Open [app.lpagent.io](https://app.lpagent.io) in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open LP Agent from your Home Screen and turn notifications on there.

## Stop notifications

Switch off **System Notifications**, or switch off single events. Pressing **Disconnect** also removes this device's subscription.
