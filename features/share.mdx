Share a position, a day or a month as a card or a short video. Sharing is available on your own positions.

## Where to share from

- **Share** in a position's menu, or the share icon when you hover its uPnL.
- Closed positions in **Historical positions**.
- The result screen after you zap out or close a position.
- The **calendar** view of Profit history: click a day, or press **Share** for a week or month.
- Copy Logs: **Share this win** on a closed copy.

### After a one-click zap out

A one-click zap out (on Robinhood Chain, a zap out from the pool page panel) opens the PnL card by itself when the close lands. If you'd rather not see it every time, tick **Don't show again** on that card. From then on you get a short notice instead, with a link to the transaction and a **Share** button that still opens the card. Untick the box there to get the card back.

The choice is saved in this browser. It doesn't change sharing on purpose, or the card at the end of the **Close** form.

## PnL card

Pick a **Format**: **Image** or **Video**.

For images, pick a **Style**:

- **Pro**: clean card with the pair, chain, protocol, PnL, invested, fees earned and time.
- **Tabby**: the same numbers, led by our mascot.
- **Chart**: the Pro card plus a price chart over the position's life, with your LP range marked.

Options:

- **Currency**: **Native** (SOL/ETH) or **USD**.
- **Privacy > Show balance**: turn it off to hide amounts. Percentages stay.
- **Invite > Add my invite link**: adds a QR code and your code to the card. Only shown when your referral link is open to new users on this chain. See [Referral](/referral).

Then **Copy** the card, **Save** it as a PNG, or **Post to X**.

On a phone, the preview shrinks to fit the screen and the options scroll, down to **Post to X**.

## Flex video

A short square video with music that replays the pool price with your range, counts up your fees and shows your PnL.

- **Show**: **Fees**, **PnL** or **Both**.
- **Currency** and **Resolution** (1080p or 720p).
- Press **Download video**. It renders in your browser, so keep the tab open until it finishes. Chrome works best.
- On a phone or tablet, **Share to X** opens your share sheet with the video attached and the caption filled in. On an iPhone you may need to tap it a second time once the video is ready.
- **Save image** saves a still picture instead.

## Period recap

From the calendar, share a **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly** recap: net PnL, win streak, win rate, number of positions and your biggest win.
