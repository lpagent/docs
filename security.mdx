## Who holds the keys

Your agent wallet's keys are created and stored by [Turnkey](https://www.turnkey.com), a wallet infrastructure provider built on secure hardware. The keys never pass through LP Agent's servers.

- **You own the wallet.** The wallet or email you sign in with is the owner of your key vault.
- **You can take the key with you.** [Export the private key](/account-and-wallet#export-your-private-key) at any time. It is decrypted inside a secure Turnkey frame, so LP Agent never sees it.
- **You can leave at any time.** Withdraw every token to any address. There are no lockups.

## What automation can do

Opening, copying and closing positions for you needs a key that can sign without asking you each time. LP Agent uses a **separate, restricted key** for this. It is not an owner of your wallet, and rules enforced by the wallet provider limit what it can sign.

**Withdrawals and key exports always need your own signature** (or an email code for email accounts).

## Protections on your trades

On top of the key rules, LP Agent adds guards you control:

- **Token filters** in **Settings > Security**: minimum market cap, 24h volume and holder count, a Jupiter organic score floor on Solana, and a token blacklist. Tokens that fail are skipped. See [Settings](/features/settings#security).
- **Meme Protection** on copies skips positions where the trusted side (SOL, USDC, USDT, ETH, WETH or USDG) is nearly empty.
- **Uniswap V4 hooked pools** are not copied unless you allow them.
- **Fund protection** (Solana) pauses copying when your copy losses hit the limit you set.
- **Take profit and stop loss** close positions at the thresholds you choose.

See [Copy Settings](/features/copy-settings) and [Managing Your Copies](/features/copy-management).

## Stay safe

<Warning>
  - LP Agent will never ask for your private key, seed phrase or a sign-in code in Discord, X or by DM.
  - Only use [app.lpagent.io](https://app.lpagent.io). Check the address before you connect your wallet.
  - Liquidity providing is risky. Pools can lose value through price moves and impermanent loss, and some tokens can go to zero. Only use funds you can afford to lose.
</Warning>
