title: "Quick Start"

---

This guide takes you from zero to your first position. It works the same on Solana and Robinhood Chain; the differences are called out where they matter.

## 1. Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to [app.lpagent.io](https://app.lpagent.io) and press **Connect Wallet** in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how you sign in">
    - **A wallet extension** such as Phantom, Solflare, MetaMask or Rabby. You approve one sign-in message: no transaction, no gas.
    - **WalletConnect** on desktop: scan the QR code with your phone's wallet.
    - **On your phone**: pick your wallet from the list (search works), or open LP Agent inside your wallet's built-in browser.
    - **Continue with email**: you get a 6-digit code by email. New email accounts choose **Solana** or **Robinhood** once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your agent wallet is created">
    New accounts get an **agent wallet** straight away. It is the wallet your positions and copies run from. See [Account & Wallet](/account-and-wallet) for how it works.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  An account belongs to one network. Sign in with a Solana wallet for Meteora pools, or with an EVM wallet for Robinhood Chain pools.
</Note>

## 2. Fund your agent wallet

Press **Deposit** in the header.

<Frame>
  ![Deposit dialog](/images/app/rh-deposit.png)
  <em>Deposit from your connected wallet, or send funds to the address shown under Receive</em>
</Frame>

- **From your wallet**: type an amount (or press **Max**) and confirm in your wallet.
- **Receive**: scan the QR code or copy the address and send from any wallet or exchange. Only send on the network shown.

| Network | What to deposit |
| --- | --- |
| Solana | SOL. Keep some SOL spare for transaction fees and position rent. |
| Robinhood Chain | ETH. ETH also pays gas. You can fund positions in ETH or USDG (set in **Settings > General > Base Token**). |

<Warning>
  Don't go too small. A tiny wallet struggles to earn back swap and gas costs, especially when copying. As a rule of thumb, copy with at least **5 SOL** on Solana or **0.08 ETH** on Robinhood Chain, then scale up once you trust the setup.
</Warning>

## 3. Choose how you want to LP

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Copy a top LP">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find a wallet">
        Open **Smart LP** and sort by all-time PnL, win rate or fees. Check the wallet's average hold time: fast flippers cost you more.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open its portfolio and press Copy LP">
        The copy dialog suggests a copy percentage from your net worth and the leader's.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set your rules and press Copy">
        Pick your size, take profit and stop loss, which pool types to follow and your protections. See [Copy Settings](/features/copy-settings).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    From then on, every new position the wallet opens is copied into your agent wallet. Track it all under **Copy LP**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Open a position yourself">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find a pool">
        Open **Pools**, filter by quote token, protocol and liquidity, and sort by fee/TVL, volume or fees.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Zap in">
        On the pool page, type an amount, pick a price range (and on Meteora DLMM a strategy), then press **Create Position** (Solana) or **Zap In** (Robinhood Chain).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Protect it">
        Set take profit and stop loss on the position, or turn on **Auto TP/SL** in Settings so every new position gets them.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    See [Create a Position](/features/create-position).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 4. Watch and manage

- **Portfolio** shows your net worth, PnL history, open positions and closed positions.
- From a position's menu you can **Increase**, **Decrease**, **Zap out**, claim fees and more. See [Manage Positions](/features/manage-positions).
- Turn on push notifications to get a ping when a copy opens, closes or fails. See [Notifications](/features/notifications).
