## TL;DR

- Copy with **at least 5 SOL of net worth** in your agent wallet
- Use the **suggested Copy Percentage** (your net worth ÷ the leader's)
- Skip leaders whose **average position age is under 18 minutes**
- Keep the token filters on: **Minimum Market Cap**, **Minimum 24h Volume**, **Minimum Holders** and **Jupiter Organic Score** (50 or higher)
- Leave **Meme Protection** on and set a **stop loss**
- Start small, then scale once you trust the wallet

This page covers Solana. For Robinhood Chain, read [Copy LP on Robinhood Chain](/tutorials/copy-robinhood-best-practices).

## 1. Copy With at Least 5 SOL of Net Worth

Every copied position pays a swap in, a swap back, and Solana fees and rent on both. That cost is roughly the same whether the position is 0.05 SOL or 5 SOL, while fee income grows with size. Below a certain size, a position can be a good trade for the leader and still lose you money.

The copy dialog shows this advice at the bottom of the step list. It is a recommendation: nothing is blocked. Lowering your copy size doesn't fix a small wallet, it just spreads the same fixed cost over less capital.

## 2. Size From the Two Net Worths

**Copy Percentage** scales every leader position. To make each copy the same share of your wallet as it is of theirs:

```text
Copy Percentage = your net worth ÷ the leader's net worth × 100
```

The dialog computes this for you and shows it as **Suggested**, with an **Apply** button. If the suggestion hits the 1% minimum, the leader is much bigger than you, and even their normal positions arrive oversized for your wallet. That's where **Max Increase Amount per Tx** helps: it caps each copied open and each copied add.

<Warning>
  A Max of **0** copies nothing. To copy without a cap, leave the field empty.
</Warning>

See [Copy Settings](/features/copy-settings#step-1-sizing).

## 3. Research the Leader

On [Smart LP](/features/smart-lp) and on the leader's portfolio, check:

- **Avg age**: wallets that hold for minutes make you pay the round trip on every flip. The copy dialog shows a **High-frequency** warning under 18 minutes.
- **Win rate and EV**: a high win rate with a negative EV means small wins and big losses.
- **Fees vs profit**: Smart LP already hides wallets whose fees are under 10% of their profit. Look for leaders whose profit is mostly fees.
- **Token style**: small caps (higher risk, higher reward) or big caps (steadier). Look at their open and closed positions.
- **Strategies**: DLMM leaders use Spot, Curve or Bid-Ask. In **What to copy** you can untick the strategies you don't want to follow.

## 4. Filter the Tokens

In **Settings > Security**, set floors that every copy must pass:

| Filter | Suggested |
| --- | --- |
| Jupiter Organic Score | 50 or higher |
| Minimum Market Cap | $500,000 or more. Raise it to a few million if you only want established tokens |
| Minimum 24h Volume | $100,000 or more |
| Minimum Holders | 100 or more |

Add tokens you never want to touch to the **Blacklist**. Copies that fail a filter show in Copy Logs with the reason.

Leave **Meme Protection** on in the copy dialog: it skips copies where the SOL, USDC or USDT side of the pool is nearly empty.

## 5. Protect Your Capital

- **Stop loss**: set one in **Exit rules**. Around 15% is a common starting point. Too tight and normal price moves close you out, and every close pays swap and network costs.
- **Fund protection**: on the Copy LP page, set a loss limit for all your copies. It pauses new copies when your closed-copy losses hit the limit, while exits keep following your leaders. See [Managing Your Copies](/features/copy-management#fund-protection-solana).
- **Smart Token Retention**: for leaders who pull and re-add the same token within a minute, this can save a sell and a buy. It also means holding the token for a short time, so only use it where the stats in the dialog show frequent re-adds.

## 6. Start Small and Scale Gradually

1. **Test**: start with a small copy percentage or a low max per transaction.
2. **Learn**: copy for 1 to 2 weeks. Check **Analysis** and **Copy Logs** to see what was copied, skipped and why.
3. **Scale**: raise the size on the wallets that work for you, and pause the ones that don't.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Copying with a tiny agent wallet**, where swap and network costs eat the fees
2. **Picking a copy percentage by feel** instead of using the suggestion
3. **Leaving Max at 0**, which copies nothing
4. **Copying a high-frequency wallet** and paying the round trip on every flip
5. **Turning off the token filters** to get more copies
6. **Not checking Copy Logs** when copies stop appearing. It is usually low balance or slippage, and the fix is one click away
