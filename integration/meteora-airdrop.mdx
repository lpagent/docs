Providing liquidity on Meteora through LP Agent counts toward Meteora's LP programs, the same as using Meteora directly. Your agent wallet is a normal Solana wallet, so any airdrop it earns belongs to you.

## Claim from LP Agent

When your wallet has MET to claim in a Meteora campaign (for example **Meteora LP Stimulus Season 2**), a banner shows on your Portfolio: **Claim X MET**. Before the claim opens it shows a countdown.

- On your own agent wallet, press **Claim Now**. The claim needs a tiny bit of SOL for the network fee.
- On any other wallet, the banner links to Meteora's claim page.

The banner disappears when the campaign's claim window ends.

## Claim on Meteora with your key

You can always claim on Meteora's site with your agent wallet:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your private key">
    Go to **Settings > Account > Export Private Key** and press **Export**. See [Account & Wallet](/account-and-wallet#export-your-private-key).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import it into a wallet">
    Import the key into Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or any Solana wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim on Meteora">
    Open Meteora's claim page, connect that wallet and claim.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Keep your private key safe. Never paste it into a website or share it with anyone, including people claiming to be LP Agent support.
</Warning>
