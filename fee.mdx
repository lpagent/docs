## Our Fee Structure

LP Agent charges one of two fees, depending on how your position is managed.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Automated" icon="robot">
    <div className="mt-2">
      <span className="text-2xl font-bold text-primary">8%</span>
      <div className="mt-2 text-sm">
        Of claimed LP fees on positions run by automation: **Copy LP**, **Take Profit**, and **Stop Loss**.
      </div>
    </div>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manual" icon="hand-pointer">
    <div className="mt-2">
      <span className="text-2xl font-bold text-primary">0.1%</span>
      <div className="mt-2 text-sm">
        Of the amount when you manually add or remove liquidity from the **pool page**.
      </div>
    </div>
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

| How the position is handled                 | Fee                      |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Copy LP                                     | 8% of claimed LP fees    |
| Auto Take Profit                            | 8% of claimed LP fees    |
| Auto Stop Loss                              | 8% of claimed LP fees    |
| Manual add / remove liquidity via pool page | 0.1% of the amount       |

**Example (automated):** A Copy LP position earns $100 in LP fees. When those fees are claimed, LP Agent takes $8. If the position earns nothing, you pay nothing

**Example (manual):** You add $1,000 of liquidity from the pool page. LP Agent takes $1
