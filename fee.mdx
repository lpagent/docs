## How fees work

What you pay depends on **who presses the button**: you, or LP Agent's automation acting for you.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="When automation acts" icon="robot">
    <div className="mt-2">

      <div className="mt-2 text-sm">
        Of the LP fees collected when LP Agent closes a position or collects fees for you: **Copy LP** following a leader, **Take Profit** and **Stop Loss**.

    <div className="mt-2">
      <span className="text-2xl font-bold text-primary">0.1%</span>

        Of the amount when you add or remove liquidity yourself: zap in, increase, decrease or zap out.
      </div>
    </div>
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

| Action | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Copy LP opens a position for you | 0% |
| Copy LP closes, reduces or claims fees when the leader does | 8% of the LP fees collected |
| Take Profit or Stop Loss closes a position | 8% of the LP fees collected |
| You add liquidity (zap in, increase) | 0.1% of the amount you put in |
| You remove liquidity (decrease, zap out, close) | 0.1% of the amount paid out |
| Compound (Solana DLMM) | 0% |
| Swaps | 0% |
| [Claim SOL](/features/claim-sol) | 0% |
| Deposits and withdrawals | 0% |

The rule follows the action, not how the position was opened. A position you opened yourself and closed by your stop loss pays 8% of its LP fees. A copied position you close yourself pays 0.1%.

**Example (automation):** a copied position earned 1 SOL in LP fees. When the leader exits and your copy closes, LP Agent takes 0.08 SOL. If the position earned no fees, you pay nothing.

**Example (manual):** you zap in 10 SOL from the pool page. LP Agent takes 0.01 SOL.
