## What is LP Agent?

LP Agent is a web app for liquidity providers. It finds pools and wallets worth following, opens positions in one click, copies the positions of top LPs for you, and closes them with take profit and stop loss while you are away.

Everything runs from an **agent wallet**: a wallet created for your account that LP Agent can act from, within strict limits. You fund it, you can withdraw from it at any time, and you can export its private key.

## Supported networks

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Solana" icon="sun" href="/integration/meteora">
    Meteora **DLMM** and **DAMM v2** pools. Positions are funded in SOL.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Robinhood Chain" icon="link" href="/integration/robinhood">
    Uniswap **V3** and **V4** pools, including tokenized stocks. Positions are funded in ETH or USDG.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Your account lives on one network. It is set by the wallet you sign in with: a Solana wallet gives you a Solana account, an EVM wallet gives you a Robinhood Chain account.
