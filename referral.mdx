## Season 1 at a glance

The referral program runs in **seasons**, separately on Solana and on Robinhood Chain. The badge at the top of the **Referral** page shows the current season and how many days are left.

- **Anyone can earn cashback.** Add a partner's code and part of the fees you pay comes back to your wallet every month.
- **Referral links are invite-only this season.** A small group of partners (KOLs) can refer new users. Links open to everyone in a later season.

<Note>
  Season 1 replaced the old flat 10% revenue share program.
</Note>

Numbers on the Referral page update every 15 minutes.

Rules for codes:

- **One code per account.** Picked the wrong one? Ask us on Discord.
- Only **partner codes** turn on cashback, and you can't use your own code.
- The code must be for **your account's network**. A Solana partner code does not work on a Robinhood Chain account, and the other way round.
- Codes are 4 to 12 letters or digits and are not case-sensitive.

## How much you get back

Cashback is a share of the LP Agent fees you pay (see [Fees](/fee)), both the 8% on automated closes and the 0.1% on manual actions. It **grows as you climb the boost tiers**.

The **My cashback** tab shows the best rate on offer this season, for example "1.6% from your first position, 3.4% at the top tier". Rates depend on the code, and the exact range for a code is shown as you type it, before you apply it.

## Boost tiers

You start at **Base** with your first position and climb from there. Your current tier, the next step and your progress are on **My cashback > Your boost progress**.

| Tier | How to unlock it | Solana | Robinhood Chain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Base** | Open your first position | - | - |
| **Boost L1** | Earn at least this much in LP fees in your first 60 days | 1 SOL | 0.05 ETH |
| **Boost L2** | Close one position of this size or more with over 10% profit | 5 SOL | 0.25 ETH |
| **Boost L3 Streak** | Keep an LP open 4 weeks in a row, counting the week you clear L2 as week 1 | 4 weeks | 4 weeks |

- A **week** runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 UTC. It counts if you had a position open at any point in it.
- **L3 is held** as long as you keep an LP open every week.
- **More than 7 days with nothing open resets you to Base.** Two weeks in a row with an LP open bring back L1 or L2; four weeks in a row bring back L3.
- Locked tiers still show your progress, so you can see how close you are.

<Note>
  The thresholds above are the Season 1 defaults. The Referral page always shows the live values for your season.
</Note>

## Payouts

| | Solana | Robinhood Chain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid in | SOL | ETH |
| Paid to | Your agent wallet | Your agent wallet |
| When | The 1st of every month (UTC) | The 1st of every month (UTC) |
| Minimum | 0.1 SOL | 0.005 ETH |

If your cashback is under the minimum, it **carries over** to next month until it reaches it. Nothing is lost.

On **My cashback** you can see:

- **Referral cashback**: this month so far, and your progress to the payout minimum.
- **All time**: everything you have earned.
- **Cashback history**: every payout with its date and amount.

Cashback accrues for up to 6 months from your first eligible position, or until the season ends, whichever comes first. Payouts can be held while an account is reviewed, and self-referrals or wash activity do not earn cashback.

## Meteora referral rewards (Solana)

LP Agent wallets are linked to Meteora's own referral program. On top of your LP Agent cashback, **Meteora pays 2% of its protocol fees on your DLMM positions, in USDC**, every Meteora cycle. The **Meteora referral rewards** card on **My cashback** shows this cycle's estimate, what is ready to claim, what you have claimed, and your total. Rewards stay claimable; nothing expires.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I signed up through a link but My cashback is empty">
    Links only attach a code if its owner is a partner in the current season on your network. Add a partner code on **My cashback** instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says my referrer is not part of the season">
    Your code belongs to someone who is not a partner this season, so there is no cashback to track. If they join, it shows up automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I used the wrong code">
    Codes can't be changed in the app. Ask us on Discord.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When does my first payout arrive?">
    On the 1st of the month after your cashback reaches the minimum (0.1 SOL or 0.005 ETH).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
